Before you scan

Download and print either picture at least 12–15 cm wide, or open it full-screen on a second device. Keep the complete image, border included. Use a flat surface, even lighting and avoid glare. On your Android phone, open the explorer in Chrome, tap Start picture AR, and allow camera access. Begin about 30–50 cm away, then move slowly until the model appears.

The website recognizes only these exact pictures—not arbitrary photos of Earth or the Sun. A picture cannot be scanned from the same phone screen that is using the camera. Camera frames are processed in your browser; this application has no image-upload endpoint.
